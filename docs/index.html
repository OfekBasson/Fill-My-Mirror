About

I'm a software engineer and researcher focused on generative AI, currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Computer Science. My work centers on making generative image models geometrically consistent across viewpoints — treating multi-view consistency as a constraint-satisfaction problem. I'm extending this interest toward agentic and scalable AI systems.

Before returning to research, I spent several years leading technical and operational work in the defense technology sector: building production software for large-scale intelligence systems, planning and coordinating complex multi-team operations, and operating high-performance, safety-critical systems that demanded precise, real-time decision-making under tight constraints. That experience shapes how I approach research — breaking down ambitious goals into rigorous, verifiable steps.
